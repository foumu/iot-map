# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple contributor covenant: be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block participation when behavior harms the project community.
